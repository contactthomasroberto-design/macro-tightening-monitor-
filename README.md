# Macro Tightening Monitor

Open, reproducible macro research stack on the synchronized tightening cycle of the Fed, the ECB
and the Bank of Japan. Work in progress — see `BRIEF.md` for the specification and milestones.

> **Disclaimer.** Educational research product. May be revised or discontinued. Not an official
> view of any central bank and not investment advice.

Quick start: `make install && make demo && make app` (offline, synthetic data).
