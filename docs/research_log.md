# Research log

Hypotheses are written **before** testing; specifications tried and results are recorded,
including failures (BRIEF §2.7). Evaluation metrics and sample splits are fixed before
looking at results. Each dashboard page states how many specifications were tried.

## Pre-registered evaluation design (applies to all forecast models)
- Metrics: RMSE, MAE; for density forecasts log score and CRPS, PIT histogram; Diebold-Mariano
  test (squared-error loss, HAC variance, Harvey-Leybourne-Newbold small-sample correction).
- Benchmarks: random walk, AR(p) with p by BIC, SPF consensus where available.
- Pseudo out-of-sample: expanding window, first forecast origin 2005-01 (monthly models) /
  2005Q1 (quarterly), horizons stated per model.
- Vintages: ALFRED / Philadelphia Fed real-time data where available; otherwise results are
  labelled "latest-vintage, not point-in-time".

## 2026-11-17: process controls (decision registry, post-mortems, forward stress limits)
Not a model change: these controls measure the process; they do not change weights.
- Decision registry: committee forecasts are recorded ex ante with a mechanical resolution rule and scored
  with the Brier score per analyst. Pre-registered use: after at least 20 resolved forecasts per analyst,
  the committee may weight analysts by skill; until then the table is information only.
- Post-mortems: threshold 1% of NAV over 4 weeks; the automatic classification (bad luck if |move| < 1.5σ
  and size within bounds; sizing if at a bound; market if beta explains > 60%; signal otherwise) is a first
  read, completed by the committee.
- Forward stress tests: nine hypothetical scenarios sized on past episodes; limits 15% of NAV and the
  benchmark's loss + 2 points. Conditional moves assume the full-sample covariance: correlations rise in
  crises, so the non-shocked moves are a central case, not a bound (H18, H23).
