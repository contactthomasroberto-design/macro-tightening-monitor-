# Note — backtests against the market

Primary sources: Diebold and Mariano (1995); Harvey, Leybourne and Newbold (1997); Chong and Hendry
(1986) and Fair and Shiller (1989) on forecast encompassing; Clarida, Galí and Gertler (2000) for the
partial-adjustment rule.

**Reading status:** standard textbook methods; papers not re-read from the build environment.

## What is replicated
Encompassing and DM tests as used in central-bank forecast evaluations.

## What is simplified, and why
The market proxy is the 1-year Treasury yield, not fed funds futures (no free history).

## What could break
A change in the T-bill/fed funds basis; a structural change in the Fed's reaction function.
